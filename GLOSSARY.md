# Dev curieux blog

The blog of Dev curieux: a French developer writing about dev things, learning in public, with English translations for a wider audience.

## Language

### Content

**Post**:
A piece of writing published on the blog, authored in French.
_Avoid_: Article, billet, entry

**Draft**:
A Post that exists in the repo but is not yet visible to readers.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, WIP

**Tag**:
A topic label attached to a Post, used to group Posts by subject.
_Avoid_: Category, label, topic

### Translation

**Translation**:
The English rendition of a Post, machine-generated from the French Post. Each Translation belongs to exactly one Post.
_Avoid_: English post, localized version, i18n version

**Stale Translation**:
A Translation whose Post has changed since the Translation was generated.
_Avoid_: Outdated translation, out-of-sync translation

### Distribution

**Syndication**:
Copying a Post or its Translation to another platform, with a canonical link pointing back to the blog.
_Avoid_: Cross-posting, republication, reposting
